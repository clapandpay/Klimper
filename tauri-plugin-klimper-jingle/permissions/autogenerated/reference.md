## Default Permission

Play the Klimper coin ding, turn the shake rattle (with haptics) on or off, tap the system haptics of a control, and let the coin pouch follow the tilt and knock.

#### This default permission set includes the following:

- `allow-play`
- `allow-set-shake`
- `allow-feel`
- `allow-pouch`
- `allow-clink`
