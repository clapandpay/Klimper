on everyone who installs an app: limits on the number of devices, digital rights management,
a ban on passing the app on. Section 10 of the AGPL forbids imposing further restrictions on
recipients, so without this permission nobody but the copyright holders could ship the wallet,
or a fork of it, through those platforms.
