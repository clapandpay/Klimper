# Contributor licence agreement (draft)

To keep dual licensing possible, every outside contribution needs a signed CLA before it is
merged. The agreement will follow the Apache Software Foundation individual CLA (a grant of
copyright and patent licence to the project, with the contributor keeping ownership). The final
text and the signing flow (a bot on pull requests) are part of backlog TASK-002. Until then,
pull requests from outside are welcome for discussion but are not merged.
