# Security

Please do not report a vulnerability in a public issue. Use GitHub's private reporting
instead: the **Security** tab of this repository, then **Report a vulnerability**. The
conversation stays private until a fix is out.

Klimper is a non-custodial wallet. The most serious reports are those that could move funds,
reveal a recovery phrase or a key, or make the approval screen show something other than what
is signed. A flaw in the Sage engine itself is also Sage's to fix: report it to
[Sage](https://github.com/xch-dev/sage) as well. Klimper has had internal review only, no
external audit.
