## Default Permission

The Klimper card through Gnosis Pay: the card address of the open wallet, signing in and out, the onboarding steps, and the account: balance, cards, payments, freezing and unfreezing a card, and showing its details on the device.

#### This default permission set includes the following:

- `allow-card-status`
- `allow-card-sign-in`
- `allow-card-sign-out`
- `allow-card-onboarding`
- `allow-card-send-email-code`
- `allow-card-register`
- `allow-card-accept-terms`
- `allow-card-answer-source-of-funds`
- `allow-card-create-account`
- `allow-card-send-phone-code`
- `allow-card-verify-phone`
- `allow-card-order-card`
- `allow-card-account`
- `allow-card-transactions`
- `allow-card-set-frozen`
- `allow-card-details`
